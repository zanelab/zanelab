> OTP (One-Time Password) component for inputting verification codes. It's usually 4 to 6 digits long and is used for two-factor authentication (2FA) or passwordless login.

### ~OTP

```html
<label class="otp">
  <span></span>
  <span></span>
  <span></span>
  <span></span>
  <input
    type="text"
    autocomplete="one-time-code"
    inputmode="numeric"
    maxlength="4"
    pattern="[0-9]{4}"
    required
  />
</label>
```
